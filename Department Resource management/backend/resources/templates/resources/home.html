Key Features of the Sharing Portal

Designed specifically for the academic and lab computing needs of students, faculty, and administration.

Centralized Digital Repository

Lecture notes, lab manuals, and previous years question papers cataloged by semester and course code for instant retrieval.

Role-Based Access Control

Strict authentication tiers: Students access registered semester materials, Faculty upload courseware, and Admin manages approvals.

Resource Request & Approval

Students can request missing academic study resources, and administrators review submissions with single-click publishing.

How It Works

Three easy steps to streamline department study materials

1

Sign Up & Choose Role

Register with your student USN or faculty ID to automatically link to your semester courses.

2

Browse & Search

Search by course code, title, module, or filter by category such as Lab Manual or PYQ.

3

Download & Collaborate

Instant PDF download, add comments for discussion, or submit new material for evaluation.
